Process

What happens next

  1. 01
    Choose a ticket type

    Use the matching button in Discord so your request reaches the correct staff area.

  2. 02
    Explain what you need

    Include useful details, dates, screenshots, or message links when they apply.

  3. 03
    Continue privately

    Authorized staff will review the ticket, respond, and close it when the matter is resolved.

Protected access

Your ticket stays private

Only the ticket creator, authorized Moderators and Administrators, the server owner, and ThyToxicBot may access the ticket.

Hidden from other members Closed records retained for six months Reports and evidence remain private

Use tickets responsibly

Reports must be truthful and made in good faith. False or misleading reports may result in moderation action. Opening a staff inquiry does not guarantee a staff position, and submitting a suggestion does not guarantee implementation.
